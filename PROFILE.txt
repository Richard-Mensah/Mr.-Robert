ROBERT KAMPI LAARI

Youth Development Advocate | Public Sector Leader | Author | Motivational Speaker | Founder, Tamale Youth Summit

Robert Kampi Laari is a Ghanaian youth development advocate, public sector leader, author, motivational speaker, and social impact entrepreneur committed to empowering young people to discover their potential, develop their leadership capacity, and create meaningful change in their communities.

He serves as the Regional Director of the National Youth Authority (NYA) in Ghana's North East Region, where he works to advance youth empowerment, entrepreneurship, digital inclusion, leadership development, peacebuilding, and sustainable livelihoods.

As Regional Director, he champions initiatives that connect young people to opportunities, strengthen youth participation in governance, promote responsible citizenship, and build partnerships among public institutions, development organisations, and private sector stakeholders.

Robert is the founder and convener of the Tamale Youth Summit, an initiative established to inspire, equip, and connect young people with the knowledge, networks, and opportunities needed to become effective leaders and change-makers. Through the summit and other youth-focused initiatives, he advocates for a generation of young people who are innovative, purpose-driven, and committed to community transformation.

An alumnus of the University for Development Studies, where he studied Development Planning, Robert brings together his background in development, leadership, community engagement, and youth advocacy to pursue practical solutions to social and economic challenges.

He is also an author whose works include If I Knew Then and Beyond Handshakes: From Potential to Proof — Networking for Career & Life Progress. Through his writing, speaking engagements, mentorship, and public engagement, he encourages individuals to embrace personal growth, cultivate meaningful relationships, develop their abilities, and pursue purposeful lives.

Robert believes that young people should not merely be beneficiaries of development but active participants in designing and delivering the solutions that shape their future.

His broader vision is to contribute to a society where talent is nurtured, opportunities are accessible, leadership is purposeful, and young people are equipped to transform their communities.

Personal motto: “See you at the top.”
